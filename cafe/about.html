Our story

How a tiny espresso bar became the neighbourhood's favourite corner.

Since 2014

A corner shop with a big heart

Sunrise started as two friends, one oven and a tiny espresso machine. Twelve years later we're still baking before sunrise and still know most of our guests by name.

  • Family-run and independent
  • Baked in small batches, never mass-produced
  • A friendly place for neighbours and newcomers
